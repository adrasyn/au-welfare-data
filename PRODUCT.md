# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The Australian general public exploring welfare spending in a federal electorate or council area, often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Find an area by postcode or name, understand recipient counts and annual expenditure estimates, and download a receipt or invoice. The UK Benefits Data site's search and exports are the confirmed reference. Maps are later work.

## Brand Personality

Clear, factual, approachable. Independent Australian branding, with no imported campaign slogans or politician identities.

## Anti-references

The UK reference's political branding is outside this independent site's approved visual scope. Avoid a map-dependent mobile interface and figures without dates or calculation explanations.

## Design Principles

- Search is the entry point on mobile and desktop.
- Counts and money appear together.
- A person may receive multiple payments; counts are not a combined unique-person total.
- Explain the difference between reported counts and estimated local expenditure.
- Use official ABS correspondence tables when geography conversion is required.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable search and exports, labelled controls, high-contrast text, touch-friendly targets, readable document previews and reduced-motion support follow the approved design.
