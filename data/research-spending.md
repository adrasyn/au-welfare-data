## CRA actual and overlap

[Productivity Commission RoGS2026, Housing and homelessness](https://www.pc.gov.au/ongoing/report-on-government-services/housing-homelessness/) reports CRA expenditure $6.4bn for 2024–25. Exact Table GA.4 Aust value: **$6,419.7m** in 2024–25 dollars, which are current dollars for that same year. Source: DSS unpublished data.

[Official GA.4 CSV](https://assets.pc.gov.au/2026-01/rogs-2026-partg-sector-overview-housing-homelessness-dataset.csv?VersionId=FfjPm2F63jFqJ1NrKxRbvtTkSqlDaebH), row56 (including header). Dimensions: GA.4 / 2024–25 / Recurrent expenditure / Commonwealth Rent Assistance / Aust / $m. Local read-only copy: `tmp/pdfs/rogs-housing-2026.csv`.

**Do not add CRA to the payment figures above.** [DSS PBS2025–26](https://www.dss.gov.au/system/files/documents/2025-03/2025-26social-servicespbsaccessible.pdf), printed p35 (PDF page45), “Cash projections for Rent Assistance”, footnote (a), explicitly states that the CRA component is included in primary income support payment, FTB or service pension figures. Decisive 19-word excerpt: “This table provides cash projections for the Rent Assistance component included in the primary income support payment”. The DSS annual report p45 also identifies CRA as a cross-program item without a standalone budget appropriation.

The PBS has per-primary-payment CRA projections, but its FY2024–25 column is **estimated actual**, published March2025; total $6,337.726m differs from final RoGS actual. It is unsuitable for subtracting components from the final actual payment totals. No final actual per-payment CRA split was identified in the audited annual report or RoGS GA.4/GA.5. YA is not split by student/other in that PBS projection table either.

Safe receipt implementation: show CRA as an informational, **non-additive** line and visibly exclude it from total arithmetic. Suggested label: “Commonwealth Rent Assistance — cross-program total; included within primary payment spending”. Explain that national CRA covers primary payments beyond the receipt’s selected programs. Do not label the entire $6.4197bn “included above”: selected receipt rows omit other CRA-paying programs, including Austudy, ABSTUDY and Special Benefit. Do not estimate CRA slices from June recipient shares.

## NDIS actual and definition

[NDIA Annual Report2024–25](https://ndis.gov.au/media/8108/download?attachment=), printed p110 (PDF page110), Note1.1G: **participant plan expenses $46,352.178m**. Cross-check: Statement of Comprehensive Income p97; audit discussion p93.

This is accrual expenditure for participant supports, including $927.547m services received in-kind. It excludes NDIA administration. It is distinct from the $45,000.879m cash participant plan expenses in the cash-flow statement p103, the Commonwealth contribution to NDIA, or forward projections. It includes supports funded through state/territory contributions as part of the national scheme. For an all-government national-benefits receipt it is the appropriate participant-support figure. The receipt must disclose this scope if it describes the result as Commonwealth-only spending.

## Arithmetic and product caveat

Sum of the ten DSS rows = $137,872.73m. Adding NDIS participant plan expenses = **$184,224.908m**. CRA is excluded from that sum because it overlaps.

This is a selected-program sum, not the Australian Government’s entire welfare budget. Actual administered expenses and the NDIS participant support accrual amount are more suitable for a expenditure receipt than Services Australia’s rounded operational payment highlights; do not mix the two definitions inside a single receipt.
